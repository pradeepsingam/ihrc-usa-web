Responsibilities

IHRC-USA Human Rights Organization, a Volunteer is a person who works willingly without receiving any salary or compensation, to protect human rights, promote social justice, and fulfill the objectives of the organization. In IHRC-USA (International Human Rights Council – USA), volunteers may be assigned important responsibilities. It is considered authorized only if it is provided officially. 1. Volunteer – Key Responsibilities Human Rights Awareness Creating awareness about human rights among the general public; participating in seminars, training sessions, and awareness events. Community Support Assisting affected people, the poor, women, children, the elderly, and marginalized communities. Human Rights Monitoring Responsibly observing and recording information about human rights violations, and submitting reports to the appropriate authorities or to the organization's authorized division. Project Activities Assisting in activities related to human rights, peace, equality, women's rights, child protection, and the environment. Administrative Support Contacting members, organizing events, preparing reports, and assisting with office work. International Cooperation Supporting international human rights events, campaigns, and the organization's authorized international activities. 2. Official Duties of a Volunteer (This applies to all volunteers) Respecting and protecting human rights. Following the organization's rules (Code of Conduct), Constitution, and regulations. Supporting the organization's objectives and policies. Creating awareness about human rights. Participating in community service activities. Responsibly reporting information about human rights violations. Helping to find ways to obtain necessary assistance for affected people. Helping to protect the rights of women and children. Promoting peace and harmony. Acting without discrimination based on race, religion, language, or gender. Assisting in organizing the organization's events with the permission of the headquarters. Participating in meetings and training sessions. Submitting activity reports. Protecting the organization's confidential information. Not misusing the organization's name. Avoiding corruption and irregularities. Working in coordination with other volunteers. Protecting the organization's reputation. Promoting human dignity, equality, and justice. 3. Actions Volunteers Must Not Do Must not issue official statements without the organization's permission. Must not collect donations or money in the name of the organization. Must not portray oneself as a legal advisor or lawyer. Must not act as an investigating officer or police officer. Must not spread unverified allegations about human rights violations. Must not share the personal information of affected persons without their consent. Must not appoint members or assign positions without the organization's permission. Must not use the organization's name for personal gain.
