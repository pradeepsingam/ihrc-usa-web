Biography

Dr. Abdur Rashid (born 1965) is a distinguished Professional Accountant, entrepreneur, and international human rights advocate whose career spans finance, diplomacy, humanitarian development, and global peacebuilding. He currently serves as the Regional Director for Canada at the US Institute of Diplomacy and Human Rights (USIDHR), High Commissioner for Canada at the International Human Rights Council (IHRCUSA), and the National President of UNIPUN Canada. Through these leadership roles, Dr. Rashid advances national and international initiatives centered on human dignity, disability rights, peace, and sustainable community development. As the Chief Executive Officer and Founder of Far West Accounting Inc., Dr. Rashid has established a respected Canadian firm recognized for delivering comprehensive accounting, financial management, and business advisory services. His entrepreneurial leadership further extends to 786 Holding Inc., where he serves as Founder and President, demonstrating his commitment to economic growth and responsible business development. Dr. Rashid’s academic achievements reflect a deep dedication to multidisciplinary excellence. He recently completed his Doctorate in Social and Human Studies (Major in Human Rights) and holds a Master of Business Administration (Major in Accountancy) from Atlantic International University. His educational background also includes Professional Accounting and Business Management studies from the London School of Business Administration, an Information Technology Management College Degree from CDI College in Quebec, and a Mini MBA from the International Business Management Institute (IBMI). He is a Licensed Professional Chartered Manager (Adm.A.) with the Ordre des administrateurs agréés du Québec (OAAQ). His professional affiliations include being an Associate Member of the AICPA, and a member of the Association des MBA du Québec. Dr. Rashid holds multiple certifications from the US Institute of Diplomacy and Human Rights, demonstrating his broad expertise in global diplomacy and human rights education. His certifications include: • Certified International Business & Protocol Consultant (2025) • Certified Diplomatic Protocol Consultant • Certified Human Rights Consultant • Certified Disability Rights Advocate • Certified Business Consultant In 2024, Dr. Rashid received the prestigious “Best Human Rights Consultant” Award from the US Institute of Diplomacy and Human Rights, in recognition of his outstanding contributions to international human rights advocacy and education. Dr. Rashid has actively engaged in international advocacy, attending the International Summit on Disability Rights 2022 in Istanbul and the Rebuilding Ukraine Forum in Bucharest, Romania, in 2023. He also attended the 18th, 19th, and 20th International Human Rights Summits in 2024, 2025, and 2026, where he was a Speaker at the UN 2026, all held at the United Nations Headquarters in New York, underscoring his commitment to advancing global disability rights and Human Rights Advocacy. Dr. Rashid's dedication to both his profession and his advocacy work continues to drive a positive impact in the fields of finance, human rights, and community development worldwide Through his combined expertise in finance, diplomacy, and human rights, Dr. Abdur Rashid continues to drive meaningful impact across Canada and around the world, championing Equal Justice for all.

Responsibilities

1. Background and Purpose The High Commissioner of IHRC-USA serves as the chief executive officer of the organization’s national administration in the United States. Under the IHRC-USA Constitution, the High Commissioner acts as the national head of the organization. This position is established to provide leadership, ensure good governance, accountability, and transparency, guide policies and programs, and advance the organization’s mission—focused on human rights, peace, and justice—at both national and international levels. 2. Objectives of the Position To provide overall leadership and guidance to the national administration. To ensure good governance, accountability, and transparency. To advance the organization’s operational mission regarding human rights, peace, and justice. To represent the organization at national and international levels. To ensure coordination among all national committees and programs. 3. Scope of Authority The High Commissioner shall possess the following authorities: To serve as the national head and hold the highest leadership position within the organization’s national administration. To act as the High Commissioner of the national administration. To preside over meetings of the National Executive Committee (NEC). To serve as the chairperson of the National Executive Committee. To provide guidance on the organization’s policies and programs. To oversee the activities of all national committees. To appoint committees as and when necessary. To make interim administrative decisions during emergencies (subject to subsequent approval by the Executive Committee). To represent the organization at national and international events. Guiding the work of the organization dedicated to human rights, peace, and justice. 4. Key Responsibilities 4.1 Submitting monthly and annual reports to the IHRC-USA headquarters. Managing the National Chapter in full accordance with the advice and guidance provided by the IHRC-USA headquarters. Fully adopting and implementing the IHRC-USA Code of Conduct and Constitution. 4.2 Administration and Leadership Serving as the National President—the highest authority within the national administration—and overseeing the overall operational direction and management of the organization. Presiding over meetings of the National Executive Committee (NEC) and determining the meeting agenda in consultation with the Secretary. Ensuring the effective implementation of decisions made by the National Executive Committee. Providing the strategic guidance and leadership necessary for the national administration. Ensuring compliance with the IHRC-USA Constitution, Bylaws, and national laws. 4.3 Policy and Programs Formulating, guiding, and reviewing national policies and programs. Developing and executing short-term and long-term strategic plans. Approving project proposals, budgets, and operational frameworks. Ensuring that national programs are aligned with the priorities of the International Human Rights Commission (IHRC). 4.4 Human Rights, Peace, and Justice Leading policies and initiatives that promote human rights, peace, and justice. Establishing investigative committees regarding human rights violations and providing recommendations. Integrating initiatives related to peace education, social justice, and legal aid. Advocating for human rights and justice before public authorities and international organizations. 4.5 Committees and Oversight Overseeing the activities of all national committees. Appointing ad-hoc and standing committees as required. Resolving disputes between national committees and ensuring coordination. Reviewing committee reports and ensuring follow-up actions. 4.6 Financial and Administrative Oversight Approving the national budget and ensuring financial discipline. Submitting audit reports and financial statements to the National Executive Committee (NEC). Ensuring the efficient use of resources and compliance with financial regulations. Making interim administrative decisions during emergencies, subject to subsequent approval by the National Executive Committee (NEC). 4.7 External Relations and Partnerships Representing the organization at national and international events. Building partnerships with donors, government bodies, UN agencies, and international non-governmental organizations. Leading fundraising and resource mobilization efforts. Serving as the primary spokesperson and advocate for the organization to the public and the media. 4.8 Monitoring, Evaluation, and Reporting Monitoring and evaluating the performance of national programs. Submitting regular operational and financial reports to the National Executive Committee (NEC) and international leadership. Ensuring the timely release of reports and organizational communications.
