Our Recognition

View all →

The International Human Rights Council–USA (IHRC-USA) is an independent, civil society organization (CSO). It is registered on the United Nations Partner Portal, and its organizational profile is featured on the Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR) website in connection with the Universal Periodic Review (UPR). Furthermore, IHRC-USA has completed the procedures for registration with the United Nations NGO Branch and is also registered on the European Commission’s EU Funding & Tenders Portal.

About IHRC-USA

The International Human Rights Council – USA (IHRC-USA) is an organization focused on human rights awareness, humanitarian values, peace, equality and community development, working through education, advocacy, community engagement and responsible cooperation.

Welcome Message

View all →

It is my great honor to present the International Human Rights Council – USA (IHRC-USA). Human rights are the foundation of a peaceful, fair, and sustainable world. Every individual, regardless of nationality, race, gender, religion, social status, or background, deserves respect, dignity, equality, and justice.
