Benefits of IHRC

  • Fundamental Protections

    Ensures fundamental protections against abuse such as torture, exploitation, cruel treatment and injustice.

  • Equality and Non-Discrimination

    Promotes equality and ensures fair treatment, protection of minority rights and freedom from discrimination.

  • Quality of Life

    Guarantees access to food, healthcare, and a safe environment for a life with dignity.

  • Protection

    Protects freedom of expression, privacy, beliefs, and the right to live in peace.

  • Empowerment and Dignity

    Helps individuals participate in public affairs and live with dignity.

  • Access to Justice

    Ensures rights and ensures that serious violations do not go unpunished.

  • Basic Needs

    Ensures access to medicine, food, water, clothing, and shelter for all.

  • Protects the Environment

    Supports the right to clean air, clean soil, clean water, and a healthy environment.

  • Protects Vulnerable People

    Protects vulnerable people from abuse and exploitation.

  • Speak Out Against Corruption

    Allows people to speak out against abuse and corruption without fear.

  • Freedom of Religion

    Protects the freedom to follow any religion or none at all.

  • Freedom to Love

    Allows people to love whomever they choose without discrimination.

  • Equal Employment Opportunities

    Promotes fair work, equal pay, and safe working conditions for all.

  • Access to Education

    Makes quality education accessible to everyone, not just the privileged.

  • Holds Governments Accountable

    Sets universal standards that hold governments accountable for human rights violations.

  • Protecting Human Dignity

    Protects people from exploitation, abuse, and discrimination.

  • Social Harmony and Peace

    Reduces conflict and promotes peaceful coexistence and cooperation.

  • Foundation for Morality and Ethics

    Builds a just society based on moral and ethical principles.

  • Promotes the Rule of Law

    Ensures fair laws, justice, and equality before the law.

  • Economic Development for All

    Promotes stability, investment, and fair economic opportunities for all.

  • Promotes Personal Responsibility

    Encourages respect for others’ rights and responsibilities in building a better society.

IHRC-USA

Promoting human rights awareness, humanitarian values, peace, equality and community development. Equal Justice for Everyone & Everywhere.
