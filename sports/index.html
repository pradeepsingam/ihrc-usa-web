Dear Members, Volunteers, Partners, and Human Rights Defenders, It is my great honor to present the International Human Rights Council – USA (IHRC-USA). Human rights are the foundation of a peaceful, fair, and sustainable world. Every individual, regardless of nationality, race, gender, religion, social status, or background, deserves respect, dignity, equality, and justice. IHRC-USA is committed to promoting and protecting fundamental human rights through awareness, education, humanitarian service, community empowerment, and international cooperation. Our mission is guided by the principles of universal justice, compassion, integrity, accountability, and respect for human dignity. Through the dedication of our members and partners around the world, we continue working toward a society where every person has the opportunity to live with freedom, safety, equality, and hope. This handbook serves as a guiding document for our organization, explaining our vision, mission, values, responsibilities, and commitment to human rights principles. I invite everyone to join hands with IHRC-USA in building a world based on peace, justice, equality, and humanity. Together, we can create meaningful change.

Members Restrictions & Regulations

53.Members Restrictions & Regulations *1. No Financial Fundraising: Members are strictly prohibited from collecting or soliciting financial financial contributions under the IHRC-USA name without explicit, written authorization from the SLK Headquarters.2. No Official Representation: No Members may act, speak, or issue statements to the media as an official international representative of the IHRC-USA without a formal mandate.3. Non-Compensated Service: All Members positions are strictly unpaid. The IHRC-USA does not provide salaries, and the headquarters is not responsible for personal expenses incurred during Members work.4. IHRC-USA ID Card Policy: Issued digital or physical Members cards remain the intellectual property of the organization. Misuse, forgery, or public leaks of these documents will lead to immediate termination.5. Strict Neutrality: In compliance with non-profit standards, Members must remain completely non-partisan. You cannot endorse political parties or candidates using the IHRC-USA affiliation.6. Content Monitoring: All content posted by Members on the organization’s network is heavily monitored. Individual accounts will be suspended if posts conflict with the core values of the IHRC-USA Headquarters.
